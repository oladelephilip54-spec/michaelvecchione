The author

Michael F. Vecchione

Hofstra Law, 1973. Assistant District Attorney in Kings County the same year. Nearly one hundred homicides. Deputy Chief of the Sex Crimes Bureau. Director of the NYPD Advocate’s Office. Chief of Homicide. Chief of the Trial Division. From 2001 to 2013, principal advisor to the Brooklyn District Attorney and Chief of the Rackets Division.

He took down judges, a political boss, Mafia cops, and a surgeon who sold bone from the dead. In 2007 the Association of the Bar of the City of New York awarded him the Thomas E. Dewey Medal.

He has taught at Hofstra, Brooklyn Law School, and St. John’s, lectured at the NYPD Academy, and advised homicide detectives. The books are not research. They are the work.

  1. 1973 Joins Kings County D.A. as an Assistant District Attorney
  2. 1980 Director, NYPD Advocate’s Office
  3. 1992 Chief of the Homicide Bureau
  4. 1994 Chief of the Trial Division
  5. 2001–13 Chief of Rackets · advisor to the D.A.
  6. 2007 Thomas E. Dewey Medal
  7. 2009– Author: Friends of the Family through Fallen Angel and Hard Guys Cry
